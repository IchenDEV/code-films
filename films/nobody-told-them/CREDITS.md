# Credits

## Source

Adapted from a talk by Theo (t3.gg). The narration paraphrases and condenses his points in third person.

## Narration

Synthesized with [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io) using library voices *Pangge* (Chinese) and *Michael Dan* (English), model `eleven_v4`. The audio files are not included in this repository; regenerate them with `./run.sh nobody-told-them tts`.
