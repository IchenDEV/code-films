# v3 structural changes: making it hit harder (draft for review)

Already approved: the Chinese text revisions (script_zh_v3_对比.md) and the new d12/d13/d14.
Below are the **new structural changes**, aimed at "speed" and "impact".

## 1. Cold open: put the question at the very start (new, about 8 s)

The film no longer starts with thunder. It starts in a dark room: on a single screen, someone types "我们是什么？". The cursor blinks, and there is no answer. Hard cut to black, then thunder → the fire. This is the hook. The audience knows from the start what the story is chasing, and when the screen finally answers near the end, the loop closes.

## 2. First dethronement: add the time scale (one new line)

After "银河系，也不过是宇宙中上千亿个星系之一", add:

> 如果把宇宙一百三十八亿年的历史压缩成一年，人类全部的文明史，只占最后的十几秒。
> If the 13.8-billion-year history of the universe were compressed into a single year, all of human civilization would fill only its final seconds.

Picture: a "cosmic year" dial. It turns all the way round, a full year, and only the last thin sliver before midnight lights up.
(Check: 5,000 years ÷ 13.8 billion years × one year ≈ 11 seconds.)

## 3. Second dethronement: add depth of time (extend one line)

> 我们与黑猩猩的 DNA，有大约百分之九十八点八是相同的。**六七百万年前，我们还有着共同的祖先。**
> … **Six or seven million years ago, we shared a common ancestor.**

## 5. Epilogue: drive the three dethronements home again

As "我们不在宇宙的中心，也不在造物的顶端，甚至不是唯一会思考的存在" is spoken, each clause gets one image:
pale blue dot → the human twig on the tree of life → the ability curve the line of light has swept past. Three hits, three blows. Then black: "我们，只是凡人。" → title.
