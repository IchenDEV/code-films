# Second-version outline and narration (Chinese and English)

Total running time is about 5 minutes. The narration sets the pace: slow speech, with 1–3 seconds of picture and music between sentences.
Visual approach: public-domain historical images (Fuxi and Nüwa on silk, the Flammarion engraving, Copernicus's diagram, Darwin's sketch, Huxley's lineup of ape skeletons, and others), slowly panned and zoomed in a documentary style, combined with the existing procedural animation (fire, starfield, cosmic zoom, bone morph, neurons, machines, screen).
The through-line of light stays: fire → starlight → microscope light → electric light → screen light.

---

## Prologue · Fire (0:00–0:25)
Picture: thunder and lightning, a fire flaring up, a group of people (slimmer silhouettes); a profile looks up at the stars.

- 很久以前，我们还不知道世界如何运转。
- 但我们已经开始，为自己寻找一个位置。

> Long ago, we did not yet know how the world worked.
> But we had already begun to look for our place in it.

## Act One · The Altar (0:25–1:00)
Picture: cave handprints → round heaven, square earth (a round dome over a square earth, the *cong* jade tube) → Fuxi and Nüwa holding the compass and set square → the Egyptian sky goddess Nut arching over the earth → the World Tree → the hands in *The Creation of Adam* → the Vitruvian Man at the center of everything.

- 天圆地方，人居其中。天、地、人，并称"三才"。
- 女娲抟土造人；神，照着自己的形象造人。
- 每一个文明，都把人放在了世界的中心。

> Heaven was round, the earth was square, and we stood between them — heaven, earth, and humankind.
> Nüwa shaped us from yellow clay. God made us in His own image.
> Every civilization placed us at the center of the world.

## Act Two · First Dethronement: Position (1:00–1:55)
Picture: Ptolemy's nested spheres (humans at the center) → the Flammarion engraving (a person poking their head through the dome of the sky) → Copernicus's 1543 diagram, with the center handed to the sun → Galileo's notebook of Jupiter's moons → zoom out to the solar system, the Milky Way, the deep cosmos → Voyager 1's pale blue dot.

- 公元二世纪，托勒密把地球放在宇宙中心。这幅图景，延续了一千四百年。
- 一五四三年，哥白尼把中心让给了太阳。
- 一六一〇年，伽利略看见木星的卫星——有些天体，根本不绕着我们转。
- 太阳只是银河中几千亿颗恒星之一；银河，也只是上千亿个星系之一。
- 一九九〇年，旅行者一号在六十亿公里外回望：地球，是一粒暗淡的蓝点。
- Title card: **第一重 · 位置** (First: Position)

> In the second century, Ptolemy placed the Earth at the center of the universe. That picture lasted fourteen hundred years.
> In 1543, Copernicus gave the center to the Sun.
> In 1610, Galileo saw moons circling Jupiter — not everything in the sky turned around us.
> The Sun is one of hundreds of billions of stars in our galaxy. Our galaxy is one of hundreds of billions more.
> In 1990, from six billion kilometers away, Voyager 1 looked back: the Earth was a pale blue dot.

## Act Three · Second Dethronement: Origin (1:55–3:05)
Picture: the Great Chain of Being (God, angels, humans, beasts, plants, stones) → the ladder collapses → Darwin's "I think" sketch grows into the tree of life → fish fin, bat wing, whale flipper and human hand morph into one another → cells dividing, the DNA double helix, the shared genetic code → Huxley's lineup of skeletons (gibbon, orangutan, chimpanzee, gorilla, human) → the human is one twig at the top of the tree, no different from the others.

- 失去了中心，我们还有出身。
- 人们曾相信，万物排成一架阶梯：神、天使、人，然后是走兽、草木、石头。人站在高处，离神最近。
- 一八五九年，达尔文出版《物种起源》。阶梯倒下，长成了一棵树。
- 鱼的鳍、蝙蝠的翼、鲸的鳍肢、人的手，是同一套骨骼。
- 几乎所有生命，都由细胞构成，共用同一套遗传密码。
- 我们与黑猩猩的 DNA，约有百分之九十八点八相同。
- 我们不是被单独放进这个世界的。我们只是生命之树上，普通的一枝。
- Title card: **第二重 · 起源** (Second: Origin)

> Without the center, we still had our origin.
> We believed all things stood on a great ladder: God, angels, humankind — then beasts, plants, and stones. We stood near the top, closest to God.
> In 1859, Darwin published *On the Origin of Species*. The ladder fell, and grew into a tree.
> A fish's fin, a bat's wing, a whale's flipper, a human hand — the same bones, rearranged.
> Nearly all life is built of cells, and written in the same genetic code.
> Our DNA is nearly ninety-nine percent the same as a chimpanzee's.
> We were not placed in this world apart. We are one ordinary branch on the tree of life.

## Act Four · Third Dethronement: Mind (3:05–4:25) (the strengthened AI section)
Picture: Descartes's diagram of the pineal gland (the seat of the soul) → Golgi-stained neurons, electrode, oscilloscope → Pascal's calculator, Babbage's difference engine → Turing's typewritten question → a chessboard (1997) → a Go board, move 37 (2016) → a protein structure folding (2020) → language, images and code flowing → a single screen.

- 失去了位置和出身，我们退到最后一处：思想。
- 笛卡尔说，我思，故我在。理性，被看作人之为人的标志。
- 后来我们看见，思想是神经元里的电与化学。
- 然后，我们开始制造会思考的东西。
- 一九五〇年，图灵问：机器能思考吗？
- 一九九七年，深蓝赢下国际象棋世界冠军。
- 二〇一六年，AlphaGo 在围棋上，走出了一步人类棋手几乎不会下的棋。
- 二〇二〇年，它预测出蛋白质如何折叠。
- 如今，它写作、作画、编程、推理——回答我们的问题。
- (On screen: the user types "我们是什么？" and the reply appears one character at a time: "你们问过火，问过星空，问过自己的身体。现在，你们在问我。")
- Title card: **第三重 · 心智** (Third: Mind)

> Stripped of place and origin, we retreated to our last refuge: the mind.
> Descartes wrote: I think, therefore I am. Reason became the mark of being human.
> Then we found that thought is electricity and chemistry, moving through neurons.
> And then, we began to build things that think.
> In 1950, Turing asked: can machines think?
> In 1997, Deep Blue defeated the world chess champion.
> In 2016, AlphaGo played a move in Go that almost no human would have played.
> In 2020, it learned how proteins fold.
> Now it writes, paints, codes, and reasons — and it answers our questions.
> (On screen: "What are we?" — "You asked the fire. You asked the stars. You asked your own bodies. Now you are asking me.")

## Epilogue (4:25–5:05)
Picture: the profile of the person at the fire and the profile of the person at the screen overlap in the same pose → black.

- 每一次，我们以为找到了人之所以特别的最后理由；每一次，知识都把它还给了自然。
- 科学所做的，也许只有一件事：把人，从神坛上请下来。
- 我们不在宇宙的中心，不在造物的顶端，也不是唯一会思考的存在。
- 我们很平凡。
- Film title

> Each time, we thought we had found the last reason we were special. Each time, knowledge gave it back to nature.
> Perhaps science has only ever done one thing: it has brought us down from the altar.
> We are not at the center of the universe, not at the summit of creation, and not the only things that think.
> We are ordinary.
