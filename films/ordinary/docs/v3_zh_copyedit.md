## New: "they are already better than most people" (draft)

Placement: in the third dethronement, after "……也开始回答我们的问题" and before the hard cut to silence. Two new shots.

### Shot A · The distribution curve (lines d12, d13)

| # | Chinese | English |
|---|---|---|
| d12 | 写作、编程，甚至制作影像——在许多事情上，它们已经胜过了大多数人。 | Writing, coding, even making films — at many tasks, they now outperform most of us. |
| d13 | 离最顶尖的人，它们还差一段距离；但这段距离，并非不可逾越。 | They still fall short of the very best. But that gap is not beyond closing. |

Picture: a thin bell curve, the distribution of human ability, in bone-white lines. A vertical line of light (AI) moves in from the left. The area it passes over fills in and the "most people" region lights up. It stops just short of the narrow tail on the right (the very best). As d13 is spoken, the line pushes forward a little and the gap narrows. Three labels below cycle: 写作 → 编程 → 影像, and the curve and the line's position shift slightly with each.
